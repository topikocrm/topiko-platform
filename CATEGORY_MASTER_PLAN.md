# Master Category Planning Document

## Executive Summary
Complete restructuring plan for Topiko Platform's category system to support a multi-vendor marketplace across India.

## Goals
1. Cover 95% of retail market needs
2. Support B2B, B2C, and D2C models
3. Enable location-based services
4. Multi-language support (English, Hindi, Regional)
5. Scalable and maintainable structure

## Hierarchy Structure: 3 Levels
```
Level 1: Main Categories (10)
Level 2: Subcategories (87)
Level 3: Products (500+)
```
